The only menubar app you'll (probably) ever need.

Clipboard history, notes, 2FA codes, scripts, battery and fan control in one panel. Music, calendar, weather and a file shelf in your notch.

What it does all day

Real screenshots, one after another. Click to skip ahead.

  1. Closed, it shows what's playing.
  2. You plug in. It shows the charger's wattage.
  3. Point at it and it opens: your song, and what's next.
  4. A meeting in a minute. Join from right here.
  5. You copied a link with trackers. One click cleans it.
  6. Claude Code needs you, and Codex is still working.
  7. Charged to 80%, where you told it to stop.
  8. The doorbell, through your headphones. Music paused.
  9. Someone's asking. Answer right here.
  10. The words of your song, under the notch.

Your music

Cover, scrubber and transport for whatever's playing. Pick which player it follows.

Weather

The temperature where you are, with the next few hours one tab away.

The next hour

Your events as bars on a timeline, so you can see if there's time for a coffee.

Files and AirDrop

Your agents

Claude Code, Codex and opencode show in the closed notch, working or waiting on you.

Notices you answer

Your charge limit, a meeting, a link with trackers. Point at one on the notch and it opens, with its buttons.

Extra apps, if you want them.

None of these are there until you install them from Settings.

Decant

App-level volume control. Turn one app down without touching the rest, mute it, boost a quiet one, or send it to different speakers. Live meters show who's making the sound.

Caffeine

Keeps the Mac awake for a set time, from one click in the footer. This button does it for real.

Focus

Fades and blurs every window but the front one, so it stops pulling your eye.

Ears On

Listens for the doorbell, knocking, alarms and other sounds while you wear headphones, and pauses your music. Nothing is recorded or sent.

ScreenLyrics

Synced lyrics for the song that's playing, in a small glass pill. Seven sizes, and it steps aside when the notch opens.

FnGestures

Hold fn and scroll for volume or brightness. Flick sideways for the next or previous track.

NotchsApp

WhatsApp in the notch, from the community. Read, reply and react from a tall notch tab, and answer new messages right on the notice.

Get it.

Free and open source. 2.6.0, macOS 26 or later.

Download for MacSource on GitHub

  1. Open the downloaded DMG and drag Oxine to Applications.
  2. Open Oxine. It's self-signed, so macOS stops it the first time: click Done, then Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy & Security.
  3. The first run walks you through color, size, the notch and your tabs. After that, ⌘⇧V opens the panel.
